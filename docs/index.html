Hippocampus · The place this brain keeps

Wildside

The hippocampus holds place cells, the neurons that fire for one spot in a remembered landscape. Wildside turns real places into living, explorable worlds.

  • WildsideFirst in-person hackathonA painted nature game that turns real places into living, explorable worlds — ecology, weather and wildlife rendered in a hand-painted style in the browser.
  • Play WildsideA painted place you can walk through, rendered live by the HAMON Engine.

Occipital lobe · Video in, understanding out

Vision

Ordinary footage turned into depth, motion, mechanism and forecast. The visual cortex is where the pathway to both eyes begins.

  • chronos-visionWatches ordinary video and forecasts where every object in the scene is going next — and which constraints will change.
  • mechanismTrack selected points in machine footage, compare possible motion constraints, and test them against later frames.
  • Phantom WindowTurns flat phone video into a window you can look around: estimated depth, tracked camera, cinematic parallax and novel views.

Temporal lobe · What happened, who said it

Memory & sound

Reconstructing the past from fragments, and putting sound where it came from.

  • afterimageHand it scraps — a blurry clip, a few photos, a partial log — and it reconstructs what most likely happened, then tells you which single observation would settle the rest.
  • CaptionverseCaptions placed in space beside whoever is speaking, with directional sound cues — accessibility that tells you who and where, not just what.
  • RecallWeaveBrowser-only adaptive learning toolkit: transparent Bayesian learner, interactive labs, lesson authoring.

Hackathons

Competition-built projects. The precentral gyrus is where intent becomes movement.

  • Replay ForgeRebuilds a real basketball play in 3D from game footage, then lets you branch the replay and ask what-if — every frame tied back to source video.
  • DominoA causal digital twin for smart-home automations: reconstruct event chains, expose rule conflicts and timing races, and prove fixes by replay.
  • TowerOpsFirst hackathon submission ever · Sept 2026An air-traffic-control conflict-awareness demo where bounded AI agents flag converging aircraft on a live radar view — built in a weekend with Strands Agents.

Parietal lobe · Independent research

Models & measurement

What would change our explanation? Workbenches that put competing models in front of a measurement.

  • WORLDLAWCompare competing models of motion, choose a revealing measurement, and see how their predictions change.
  • Kernel GardenGenerate competing CUDA kernels, run them on a real GPU, and drop any that fail independent numerical checks.

Left eye · the fiction world

Spire of Octaves

A vertical world born from an unfinished sacred song: cities stacked on cities. Below the bells lies Geargrave, the working dark of the Spire.

  1. Tolman’s WalkThe great shift-wheel turns over the bearing house: “the wheel as government”.
  2. The weld-trunks“Vertical arteries carrying workers, hot freight, pipes, hoists, and shift bells.”
  3. The forge chapels“Forge-orange caught behind black shrouds.”
  4. The boiler rows“Narrow attached rooms along warm mains”, and boiler-stew held at working temperature.
  5. The cold light“Black girders crossed by frost-pale geometry; no source for the cold light.”

A lore-true Geargrave render is in progress; the image above is an earlier Spire interior study.
