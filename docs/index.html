02Browser-only / local-first utility

ReturnBy

Paste your own order email, correct the extraction, track multiple due dates and export calendar reminders.

Runs the repository parser, merchant policy table, deadline math and RFC 5545 export. Review every field, edit the fallback, filter saved orders and keep all approved data in this browser; the pasted email is never persisted.

03Synthetic airspace / live decision-support workbench

TowerOps Airspace Lab

A traffic-management simulation where you create flights, change their paths and see where conflicts develop.

What it does under the hood

A planner proposes adjustments; each simulated change goes through approval and readback, with an audit trail. The actual AdvisoryPlanner and ControlRoom run in-browser via CPython/WebAssembly, not a mock or replay. It uses synthetic or visitor-provided data and is not operational air-traffic-control software.

How it gets built

AI agents carry out whatever I direct, software included. I set the bar and decide what ships.

  1. 01

    I pick the work

    A research report, an ops problem, or software that needs building. Whatever needs doing.

  2. 02

    I set the bar

    A short brief: what it must do, what it must not do, and how we will know it works.

  3. 03

    Agents carry it out

    AI agents carry out the task I direct, whether it is software, research reports, or operations work.

  4. 04

    Checks before claims

    I check the result against the bar. Tests and CI matter for software; other work needs its own evidence.

  5. 05

    I decide what ships

    Software links to source where it is public. Reports and ops work ship when I decide they clear the bar.

More work

About

I study computer science and physics at Cal State Long Beach, with a minor in biology. I'm a BUILD Scholar working toward MD/PhD training.

I direct AI agents across more than software: running a home estate of several machines, producing research reports, disk and ops cleanup, and building this site. I set the bar and decide what ships.

My research experience spans gait and rehabilitation, behavioral neurobiology, protein molecular dynamics, and collaborative surgical-ergonomics work. I'm interested in measurement, reproducible analysis and scientific software.

Checks come before claims. I keep runnable examples, source and explicit limits with the work. Research contributions are collaborative; synthetic demos are not evidence of clinical validity, learning gains or savings on someone else's data.
