Independent investigations

WORLDLAW

What measurement would distinguish two explanations of the same motion?

An executable torque-model workbench that compares candidate explanations, proposes a discriminating measurement, and replays predictions.

Inside the study

The virtual instrument begins with small-angle data that fit both spring and gravity models. A proposed wide-angle measurement changes the selected model to gravity; withheld predictions update. Motion Lab also uses a declared fixed pivot and a tracked video marker to produce angular observations, retaining gaps and corrections.

Finite seven-term scalar torque library; default experiments are synthetic. Video tracking requires declared calibration and does not establish physical laws.

Kernel Garden

Can a faster implementation survive independent correctness checks?

Generate CUDA candidates, compile and measure them on a real GPU, reject incorrect variants, and export the measured winner.

Inside the study

Retained trials cover float32 vector addition on one RTX 4070. Bounded block-size search adds a held-out size; an exported winner was separately compiled and checked with new inputs. A complementary CPU arena explores compiled AXPY variants.

One GPU device and one GPU operator; parameter search, not general algorithm invention or an established speedup over vendor libraries.

Computational memory

When should a system revise what it remembers?

Retrieval-plasticity and consolidation experiments test how records are reweighted and when repeated episodes justify reusable lessons.

Inside the study

Bounded per-record strengths reorder retrieval within fixed trust tiers. A separate consolidation prototype preserves raw records and evaluates proposed lessons on chronological holdouts, retaining conflicting interpretations rather than overwriting them.

Offline and synthetic replays; some mechanisms remained staged at the recorded study checkpoint. Not a biological memory model or an established learning-gain result.

Applications, research tools, and smaller interactive experiments beyond the engine.

Phantom Window

What is just outside the camera’s view?

A video records one camera’s path. Phantom Window lets you explore nearby viewpoints: lean away from the original framing, inspect an edge, and compare what changes.

Current scope

The prototype estimates depth and camera motion, reuses pixels across frames, and renders nearby viewpoints. Source comparison and pixel provenance keep the reconstruction open to inspection.

Nearby views contain estimated geometry and inferred regions.

Captionverse

What if a caption had a place in the scene?

A line can be quiet, shouted, interrupted, or spoken behind someone else. Captionverse explores what changes when those differences affect the presentation of the words.

Current scope

The local-video workbench combines editable subtitles, speaker-linked layouts, experimental depth-aware text, phrase recall, and interval replay. Original subtitles and reviewed revisions stay separate.

Automatic transcription and placement remain drafts to review.

DOMINO

Why did the door unlock when nobody was home?

A familiar visitor rings. One rule welcomes them; another should protect an empty house. DOMINO makes the resulting event chain small enough to reason about.

Current scope

Replay the modeled observations, inspect rule guards and competing commands, reduce the counterexample, and compare repairs against behavior that should still work.

Invented events and finite model checks; no real-home connection.

Replay Forge

Could you watch the possession from above?

A broadcast camera catches the possession as it happens. Replay Forge explores that movement through a navigable court model and lets an alternative play branch beside it.

Current scope

A licensed 19-second basketball excerpt supplies person and ball detections. A calibrated court, free camera, source inset, and saved branches support comparison without overwriting the original.

Tracking is fragmented. Alternate plays are authored, not predictions.

About this room
