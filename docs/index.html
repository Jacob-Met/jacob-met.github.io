RecallWeave

A browser-only biology lesson that picks your next question by expected information gain and shows its mastery estimates as you answer.

The learner model is plain Bayesian knowledge tracing with every probability visible; there is no chatbot and nothing leaves the page. Submitted to ForgeHacks 2026 (Devpost entry 1221781).

02Browser tool · TypeScript

ReturnBy

Paste an order-confirmation email and get the return deadline plus a calendar reminder, parsed entirely in your browser.

Rule-based extraction of store, date, order number and total, a per-store return-window table, and an RFC 5545 calendar file with a reminder three days out. Try a sample: the emails are fictional.

TypeScript · Vite · localStorage only

How it gets built

AI agents carry out whatever I direct, software included. I set the bar and decide what ships.

  1. 01

    I pick the work

    A research report, an ops problem, or software that needs building. Whatever needs doing.

  2. 02

    I set the bar

    A short brief: what it must do, what it must not do, and how we will know it works.

  3. 03

    Agents carry it out

    AI agents carry out the task I direct, whether it is software, research reports, or operations work.

  4. 04

    Checks before claims

    I check the result against the bar. Tests and CI matter for software; other work needs its own evidence.

  5. 05

    I decide what ships

    Software links to source where it is public. Reports and ops work ship when I decide they clear the bar.
