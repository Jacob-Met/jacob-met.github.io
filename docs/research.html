Research contribution · 2026

Movement & rehabilitation

I've been a student researcher with gait rehabilitation research teams since March 2026. I help during participant sessions while people walk through the motion-capture room, then label markers, organize trials, and check the data before analysis.

I also fixed a MATLAB gait-analysis workflow that had been ported to Python: unit mismatches, gait event detection, and making it error out instead of returning a wrong number. I checked it against representative trials before handing it off.

My part of a team project; the clinical questions and results belong to the lab.

Surgical ergonomics

I contributed to a research collaboration on surgeon ergonomics in urologic surgery.

A research collaboration, not clinical practice. No patient data or unpublished results appear here.

Research experience · 2025–26

Proteins & molecular dynamics

I was a remote student researcher with the Joshi Laboratory at Georgetown from September 2025 to April 2026. Most of it was data work: finding molecular-dynamics trajectories, cleaning them, and converting them to one format.

I computed features like PCA and RMSF, and wrote checks for AI-generated protein structures (RMSD, radius of gyration, bond geometry) to catch ones that look fine and are physically wrong. I also turned papers from the field into validation plans the group could test against.

Computational research and prototypes, not drug discovery or a biological result.

Research experience · 2025

Behavior & neurobiology

I was in the Tsai Lab at CSULB from January to September 2025. The lab studies sex differences in brain and behavior.

I scored mouse behavior from video (automated tracking first, then checked by hand), analyzed data in GraphPad Prism, and helped with mouse handling, genotyping and tissue collection.

Supervised work in someone else's lab; the lab's results and publications are its own.
