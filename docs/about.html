The person behind the work

Jacob Metoyer.

Researcher, builder, writer as M. Schauz, and cosplayer as tornadocos.

I don't want to build a life around making all my interests look like one job description.

Biology, medicine, and computation are enduring centers for me. I care about understanding living systems and hope to pursue an MD/PhD. I also make software, create the Spire of Octaves world, write fiction, build cosplay props, and spend time with anime, manga, and games.

Sometimes those interests meet. Research needs better software. A costume starts with a character and becomes a modeling problem. A fictional city becomes more convincing when I think seriously about the work that keeps it alive. Sometimes an interest is simply worth pursuing on its own.

One person, several names.

Jacob Metoyer is my public name. M. Schauz is my fiction byline. tornadocos is my cosplay handle, and TornadoZW is my MyAnimeList username. They point to the same person, without requiring every space to speak in exactly the same voice.

What this site is for.

This is an evolving record of what I have made, contributed to, tried, and become interested in. It is not intended to pretend that every experiment succeeded, every draft is finished, or every project belongs under one specialty.

I want the work to be traceable to me and the process to be legible. I use AI openly. I also want the differences between direction, implementation, collaboration, source material, and validation to remain visible.

What I hope comes next.

More serious research. Better tools. Fiction worth reading. Worlds worth inhabiting. Things made with care. I expect the shape of the work to change as I learn; the site should have room for that.
