About

About me.

The longer version, in order.

I’m studying computer science and physics at Cal State Long Beach, with a minor in biology. I’m a BUILD Scholar, and I speak English and Spanish.

My first lab was the Tsai Lab at CSULB, in 2025. The lab studies how sex differences show up in the brain and in behavior. I worked with the mice, helped with genotyping, and scored their behavior from video, going back over the automated tracking by hand.

After that I spent about eight months working remotely with the Joshi Laboratory at Georgetown on protein simulations. Most of it was getting simulation data into shape and checking whether computer-generated protein structures were physically possible.

Since March 2026 I’ve been doing gait rehabilitation research. I help during participant sessions and get the data ready for analysis. I also fixed an analysis workflow so it stops with an error instead of giving a wrong answer, and I helped on a study of the physical strain surgery puts on surgeons.

The results from all of that belong to the labs, so they aren’t on this site.

Outside the labs I do my own research, mostly on how people and AI systems work together and when that actually helps. I also write software, and a few of those projects are public. Most of my own research isn’t published, so you’ll only find the parts that are.

After my degrees I’m hoping to go on to an MD/PhD. The rest of the time it’s cosplay, music, anime, manga and games.

Short bio

Jacob Metoyer studies computer science and physics at California State University, Long Beach, with a minor in biology, and is a BUILD Scholar. He has worked in labs studying behavioral neuroscience, protein simulations and gait rehabilitation, does independent research on how people and AI systems work together, and plans to pursue an MD/PhD.
