Personal practice

Cosplay & physical making

I cosplay as tornadocos. A build starts from a character and quickly becomes a set of practical problems: how armor should sit on a real body, how a prop holds together, which parts are worth modeling in Blender and printing.

I keep a working archive of armor, prop and 3D-printing files. Not every file in it has become a finished piece. Instagram is where the finished work goes.

The characters and their designs belong to their original creators.
