Built through the work

HAMON Engine is the creation and simulation engine within HAMON, Jacob Metoyer’s company and independent body of work. Its Rust runtime combines agent-facing interfaces, an ECS, physics and rendering, with native and browser targets.

Agents can author and inspect systems through typed commands and compare runs through deterministic replay. The engine source remains private; its public outputs let you inspect what it produces.
