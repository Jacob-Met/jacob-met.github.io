    The company

    HAMON 刃文

    One company that takes a rough seed and grinds it, iteration by iteration, into something real: tools for AI and experiences for people.

    • Wildside First in-person hackathon: A painted nature game that turns real places into living, explorable worlds — ecology, weather and wildlife rendered in a hand-painted style in the browser.
    • Hamon Engine: A Rust + wgpu game engine built for agent control: typed ECS commands, deterministic replay, native and browser targets.
    • Hamon model research: Agents running real experiments on new model architectures, representations and learning algorithms designed for clusters from the start.
    • Hamon memory research: How should an agent decide what to remember, revise or forget? Working prototypes, Python with a Rust core.

    The fiction world

    Spire of Octaves

    A vertical world of cities stacked on cities — go down to be born, go up to be remembered. Thirty-seven free stories across nine heights of the Spire, from the boiler-row Clans of Geargrave to the brine courts of the Salt Scriptoria, plus serials, a short novel on Royal Road, and a game in progress on the Hamon Engine.

    • Spire of Octaves: The project page: the world, the heights, the long works and the game.
    • Where to begin: Salt’s End, then What Strays and The Shelf Must Eat — an annotator from the Salt Scriptoria goes down the ladder-streets to read a wall nobody else can.
    • The game: You play a copyist who cannot speak, and every page you commit leans the world one way. Being built in the Hamon Engine.

    Agents that act, with a human in charge

    Decisions

    Software that plans and handles real errands — money, meals, paperwork — and stops to ask before anything irreversible.

    • TasteTable: Agentic weekly dining and outing planner for seniors and caregivers, grounded in Qloo taste signals.
    • Ledgerly: Agentic invoice and payout copilot for freelancers with a human approval gate.
    • ScopeSignal: Evidence-backed milestone checkout with deterministic PayPal Sandbox replay.
    • workflow-checks: Evidence-first back-office checks on synthetic data: freight detention, invoice audit, PT authorization, utility exceptions.

    Seeing how things connect

    Systems & space

    Making invisible systems legible: cause and effect in a smart home, aircraft converging in a sky, a running program you can walk inside.

    • Domino: A causal digital twin for smart-home automations: reconstruct event chains, expose rule conflicts and timing races, and prove fixes by replay.
    • synapse: Walk around inside a live, running software system in XR — see processes and data move, reach in and change them.
    • TowerOps First hackathon submission ever · Sept 2026: An air-traffic-control conflict-awareness demo where bounded AI agents flag converging aircraft on a live radar view — built in a weekend with Strands Agents.
    • Threshold: A doorbell that works for people who can't easily hear, see or reach the door: who's there, what they want, and a way to answer.

    What happened, who said it

    Memory & sound

    Reconstructing the past from fragments, putting sound where it came from, and helping people hold on to what matters.

    • afterimage: Hand it scraps — a blurry clip, a few photos, a partial log — and it reconstructs what most likely happened, then tells you which single observation would settle the rest.
    • Captionverse: Captions placed in space beside whoever is speaking, with directional sound cues — accessibility that tells you who and where, not just what.
    • RecallWeave: Browser-only adaptive learning toolkit: transparent Bayesian learner, interactive labs, lesson authoring.
    • Familiar Memories: When a relative visits, it recognizes them and brings up the photos you share — with consent and revocation built in, for families living with memory loss.

    Video in, understanding out

    Vision

    Ordinary footage turned into depth, motion, mechanism and forecast.

    • chronos-vision: Watches ordinary video and forecasts where every object in the scene is going next — and which constraints will change.
    • Phantom Window: Turns flat phone video into a window you can look around: estimated depth, tracked camera, cinematic parallax and novel views.
    • mechanism: Recovers the joints, linkages and working dynamics of real machinery from nothing but a video of it moving.
    • materialtruth: Lights a part from many controlled angles and reads the surface back, catching scratches and defects a single photo misses.

    Bodies, machines, measurement

    Movement

    Athletes, surgeons and robots in motion, and the instruments that capture it.

    • Replay Forge: Rebuilds a real basketball play in 3D from game footage, then lets you branch the replay and ask what-if — every frame tied back to source video.
    • Surgical ergonomics: Tracking surgeons' posture, muscle load and heart rate during procedures, with a realtime demo — built for the Soangra Lab at UCI.
    • Pneumatic MRI sim: A deterministic Rust simulation of the control stack for an air-driven surgical robot that works inside an MRI scanner.
    • CaptureSuite: Windows-first multimodal research capture: daemon, versioned worker plugins, sealed sessions, PySide6 operator UI.
