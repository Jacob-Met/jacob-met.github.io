Independent AI systems research

I build setups where one or several AI models do real tasks, then check what they got right, where they failed, and whether adding more models was worth it.

Every run saves the task, what the models did, and what went wrong, so the result can be checked later instead of taken on trust.

Independent work, outside school and any lab, and not peer reviewed.

What I’m testing

Mostly two things. Whether running more models at once actually helps, or just adds coordination overhead. And how much context a task really needs.

To keep it fair I run the same task with one model, a small group and a larger setup, and I keep the failures next to the successes.

Next

I want to try this with an organization: look at how a team does something now, build an AI-assisted version with ordinary tools like ChatGPT, and measure quality, time and cost. I haven’t done this for a client yet.
