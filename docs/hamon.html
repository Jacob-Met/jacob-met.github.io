01 · CORE TECHNOLOGY

The HAMON Engine

An AI-native engine for everything.

The HAMON Engine is meant to cover games and worlds, simulation, authoring, rendering, and science and engineering tools, and to be driven directly by AI agents as well as by hand. It is a Rust and wgpu workspace of 27 crates that runs natively and in the browser through WebAssembly and WebGPU.

  • Built for agents

    One method table served as a command line, JSON-RPC and MCP, so AI agents and tools can build, inspect, step and test the engine with no window. Typed deterministic runtime commands, replay, and SHA-256 experiment receipts make what an agent did checkable. The native editor and inspectors are optional clients.

  • Deterministic runtime

    An archetype ECS with a work-stealing job system, a fixed timestep and seeded randomness, so a run can be replayed from its recorded input. A typed CPU runtime sits on top.

  • Physics and animation

    Deterministic rigid-body physics with hinges, capsules and triangle surfaces. Skeletal animation with blending, IK, retargeting and skinning.

  • Audio, input, scripting and networking

    A deterministic audio mixer and cue format, backend-neutral input mapping, a sandboxed WebAssembly scripting host, HamonLang (an experimental systems language with a static access checker) and lockstep input-frame networking.

  • Assets and authoring

    Diffable text scenes and memory-mapped cooked packs, glTF import and an asset journal. Editable half-edge mesh authoring and node-graph materials that compile to WGSL.

  • Procedural worlds and creatures

    Code-only sculpting from signed-distance shapes: characters, birds, insects, trees, flowers, rocks and whole scenes, with no external modelling tools.

  • Rendering and style

    A forward PBR real-time renderer with cascaded sun shadows, point lights and height fog, plus headless and hardware-qualified capture. A style system with 17 selectable looks, from hand-painted luminous cel with ink line art to watercolor and graphic ink.

In progress. The engine’s AI-native authoring roadmap is open work, not shipped: live authoring connected to rendering, material graphs executed in the scene renderer, procedural modeling that updates only the affected geometry, a high-quality final-image workflow, and discovery of tools and reusable assets.

The engine repository is private. The native editor is a prototype, and larger renderer goals such as global illumination are planned, not done. What you can see today is published through Wildside, Lanternwake and the recorded forge on the homepage; those are snapshots, not the whole engine.

02 · WORLDS AND GAMES · FLAGSHIP

Wildside

▶ Play Wildside

Real places you care about, turned into living, hand-painted worlds you explore at human, bird and insect scale, rendered live by the HAMON Engine in your browser. Its default look is hand-painted, luminous cel shading with ink line art; the engine’s style system offers sixteen others.

03 · RESEARCH INSTRUMENTS

Instruments for looking closely

Evidence-first tools that share one approach: show what was observed, keep the uncertainty, and refuse to overclaim. These are early. The repositories are private and have no public demo yet.

  • Afterimage

    A physical-world evidence engine. It reconstructs what is known and what might be true from fragmentary media, keeps competing explanations, and says what to observe next.

  • Mechanism

    A motion lab for machinery. Track markers in real video, compare image-plane paths, and fit constrained kinematic models recomputed from saved evidence.

  • Synapse

    A spatial observatory for real running computational systems. The first slice is a local, read-only browser instrument for a live process tree.

  • Chronos Vision

    A temporal observatory for video. It contrasts uncalibrated future-trajectory hypotheses and replays past forecasts against later frames. An experimental first release.

  • Microverse

    An early investigator for microscopic life: follow individuals through supplied detections and keep competing explanations of an encounter.

  • MATERIALTRUTH

    A photometric inspection desk for surfaces. It infers relative albedo and normals from multi-illumination images and refuses where the data cannot support a result.

04 · AGENTS AND WORKSHOP

The rest of the estate

The tooling that lets one person run all of this. Both are private and early.

  • HAMON agent platform

    Early work on a supervised agent runtime: durable claims, recovery and replay, and memory with explicit rights. Built as deterministic fixtures; not a deployed product.

  • HAMON Workshop

    A simulated workshop operated through MCP: discover, reserve and run a virtual workcell, keep failed actions, and recover from the last committed pose. A software foundation for physical capability, not hardware yet.
