For teams

Have a workflow worth untangling?

I build small tools and test AI-assisted workflows. If your team has a specific bottleneck in research data, document handling or a repetitive operation, I’m interested in seeing whether a focused pilot would help.

Start with the actual problem

Tell me what your team does now, where it gets stuck, and what a useful result would look like. I’ll suggest a narrow first step: the input, the output, the checks, and how we would tell whether the change helped. If the existing tool already does the job, I’d rather say that than build a duplicate.

A small, testable pilot

The first pass could use sample or redacted material. We’d agree on data handling, scope, access and terms before using real records. The deliverable would be a working example and a short account of what passed, what failed and what still needs a person.

What you can inspect

My public software includes CaptureSuite, a multi-device research capture project. My AI-systems page explains how I compare assisted workflows without hiding failed runs. Those are examples of how I work, not case studies from your organization.

Start a conversation

Send me a short note on LinkedIn with the workflow, the kind of information involved (not the information itself), and what you’d want a pilot to show. Please don’t send participant data, passwords or confidential files in a first message.
