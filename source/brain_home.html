Left eye · the fiction world

Spire of Octaves

A vertical world born from an unfinished sacred song: cities stacked on cities. Below the bells lies Geargrave, the working dark of the Spire.

  1. Tolman’s WalkThe great shift-wheel turns over the bearing house: “the wheel as government”.
  2. The weld-trunks“Vertical arteries carrying workers, hot freight, pipes, hoists, and shift bells.”
  3. The forge chapels“Forge-orange caught behind black shrouds.”
  4. The boiler rows“Narrow attached rooms along warm mains”, and boiler-stew held at working temperature.
  5. The cold light“Black girders crossed by frost-pale geometry; no source for the cold light.”
